# Screenshot

The Next HiCar screenshot feature relies on the Driving Mode feature on your phone. If you are not yet familiar with HiCar Driving Mode, see [Introduction to Driving Mode](../dmode/driving-mode.md).

When Next HiCar is connected and you turn on Driving Mode on your phone, the Driving Mode interface turns into a Next HiCar remote control as shown below. Tap the "Screenshot" button to capture the current HiCar screen — the image is saved to your phone's photo album.

With Next HiCar connected, you can reach the screenshot feature in either of these ways:

1. Tap the HiCar pop-up in the Live Window at the top of the phone screen.
2. Go to "Settings > Multi-Device Collaboration > HUAWEI HiCar > Driving Mode".
