# Entering Driving Mode

After updating to a Next HiCar version that supports Driving Mode, follow these steps:

Settings > Multi-Device Collaboration > HUAWEI HiCar > Driving Mode

<img
  src={require('./img/enter-driving-mode-1.jpg').default}
  width="400"
/>

Once you do that, you will enter Driving Mode.
